MONOPOLY DEAL — RULES
Source: monopolydealrules.com (General, How to Play, Payment, Property, Action,
Rent, Just Say No, Deal Breaker, House & Hotel, Cards & Contents sections).

OBJECT OF THE GAME
Be the first player to have THREE complete property sets, of different or the
same colors, laid out in front of you. Each property card states how many
cards that color needs to be a complete set.

THE DECK: 110 cards total — 4 "Quick Start Rules" cards (removed before play),
28 property cards, 11 property wildcards, 13 rent cards, 34 action cards,
20 money cards.

SETUP
Remove the 4 Quick Start Rules cards. Shuffle. Deal 5 cards to each player.
Put the rest face down in the center as the draw pile.

A TURN
1. Draw 2 cards from the draw pile at the start of your turn and add them to
   your hand. (If you start your turn with NO cards in hand, draw 5 instead
   of 2.)
2. Play up to 3 cards. You do not have to play any. A "play" is placing a card
   in one of three places:
     - your BANK (money cards, or action cards played as money), face up
     - your PROPERTY area (property and property wildcard cards), face up
     - the center DISCARD PILE (an action card played for its action)
   Playing an action card for its action, playing it as money into your bank,
   and playing a property all each use one of your 3 plays. Double The Rent
   uses a play of its own (see Rent).
3. End your turn. You may not have more than 7 cards in hand at the end of
   your turn; discard extras face up onto the discard pile.

Play proceeds clockwise. The game ends the moment a player has 3 complete
sets in front of them.

MONEY / THE BANK
Money cards and action cards can be banked as money. Once a card is in your
bank or property area it is on the table and cannot go back to your hand.
Property cards are NEVER "money" and cannot be banked, but they can be used to
pay debts.

PAYMENT (Debt Collector, It's My Birthday, Rent, etc.)
- You choose which of your cards to pay with — from your bank, your properties,
  your houses/hotels, or any combination, in any order. The person collecting
  does not get to choose.
- You may only pay with cards on the table in front of you. Cards in your hand
  can never be used to pay.
- No change is given. If a debt is $3M and you only have a $5M card, the
  collector keeps the whole $5M.
- If you do not have enough to cover the full amount, you pay everything you
  have on the table and the rest of the debt is forgiven.
- If you have nothing on the table with monetary value, you pay nothing.
- Property cards used as payment go into the collector's PROPERTY area.
- An action card received as payment goes into the collector's BANK and can
  only be used as money after that (its action is spent).

PROPERTIES
- Play property cards face up in front of you. Group them by color.
- You win with any 3 COMPLETE sets, even if two or three are the same color.
- Property wildcards count as the color(s) shown. Two-color wildcards can be
  flipped/reassigned between their two colors. The 10-color "multicolor"
  wildcard can stand in for any color.
- You may move and reassign your wildcards freely, but only during your own
  turn. Exception: a property received via Forced Deal can be placed where you
  want when you receive it.
- The multicolor wildcard has NO money value: it cannot be banked, cannot be
  used to pay, and you cannot charge rent on it unless it is grouped with at
  least one real property card of that color. It can be taken by Sly Deal /
  Forced Deal (if not in a complete set) or Deal Breaker (if in a complete
  set).
- A set can never hold more cards than the number needed to complete it. If
  wildcards give you extras, the surplus must start a new set of that color.

ACTION CARDS (each can instead be banked as money)
- PASS GO: draw 2 extra cards from the draw pile.
- DEBT COLLECTOR: force ONE player of your choice to pay you $5M.
- IT'S MY BIRTHDAY: EVERY other player pays you $2M.
- SLY DEAL: take 1 property card (or wildcard) from any opponent. It may NOT
  be taken from a complete set.
- FORCED DEAL: swap one of your property cards for one of an opponent's.
  Neither card may come from a complete set. Equal value is NOT required.
- DEAL BREAKER: take an entire COMPLETE set from an opponent, including any
  house and hotel on it. Cannot target an incomplete set — if you try, the
  card is just discarded and you lose that play.
- HOUSE / HOTEL: see House & Hotel.
- JUST SAY NO: see Just Say No.
- RENT / DOUBLE THE RENT: see Rent.

RENT
- A rent card is played to the discard pile, and you must have property of the
  matching color on the table to charge rent.
- You pick ONE color for each rent card played, and charge the standard rent
  for however many properties you have in that color (see chart below).
- Two-color rent cards: every opponent pays you.
- 10-color "wild" rent cards: only ONE opponent of your choice pays you.
- DOUBLE THE RENT: play it together with a rent card to double that rent. It
  costs one of your 3 plays (so a rent + one Double The Rent = 2 plays; you
  can even play two Double The Rent cards on one rent if you have the plays,
  quadrupling it).
- A Just Say No on a Double The Rent removes the doubling.

HOUSE & HOTEL
- A HOUSE can only be added to a COMPLETE set. It adds $3M to that set's rent.
- A HOTEL can only be added to a complete set that ALREADY has a house. It
  adds a further $4M to the rent.
- Houses and hotels cannot be placed on Railroad or Utility sets.
- On a complete set, a house/hotel can only be taken with Deal Breaker, not
  Sly Deal or Forced Deal. If a house or hotel is sitting alone on the table
  (because the set under it was broken up to pay a debt), it CAN then be taken
  by Sly Deal or Forced Deal.
- If you break up a complete set that had a house/hotel (e.g. to pay rent),
  the house/hotel stays on your table on its own until you complete another
  set to move it onto.

JUST SAY NO
- Cancels an action used against you (rent, Sly Deal, Forced Deal, Deal
  Breaker, Debt Collector, It's My Birthday, etc.).
- Can only be played in response to an action another player targets at you —
  or to cancel another Just Say No.
- Does NOT count toward your 3 plays for the turn, whether you play it on your
  own turn or someone else's.
- A second Just Say No cancels the first one (the original action goes
  through). A third cancels the second, and so on — no limit.
- It only protects the player who plays it. If someone plays It's My Birthday,
  your Just Say No excuses you but the other players still pay.
- Must be laid onto the discard pile to be played.

STANDARD REFERENCE — set sizes and rent (widely published values; the site
above confirms the rules but not this chart)
  Color        Cards to complete   Rent by # of properties (1 / 2 / 3 / 4)
  Brown ................ 2 ........ 1 / 2
  Light Blue .......... 3 ........ 1 / 2 / 3
  Pink (Magenta) ...... 3 ........ 1 / 2 / 4
  Orange .............. 3 ........ 1 / 3 / 5
  Red ................. 3 ........ 2 / 3 / 6
  Yellow ............. 3 ........ 2 / 4 / 6
  Green .............. 3 ........ 2 / 4 / 7
  Dark Blue .......... 2 ........ 3 / 8
  Railroad .......... 4 ........ 1 / 2 / 3 / 4
  Utility .......... 2 ........ 1 / 2
  House on a complete set: +3M rent.  Hotel (on top of a house): +4M rent.

STANDARD REFERENCE — card money values (bank value if played as money)
  Property: Brown 1M, Light Blue 1M, Pink 2M, Orange 2M, Red 3M, Yellow 3M,
    Green 4M, Dark Blue 4M, Railroad 2M, Utility 2M.
  Multicolor (10-color) property wildcard: 0 (no value).
  Rent card: 1M (two-color) / 3M (10-color wild rent).
  Pass Go 1M, It's My Birthday 2M, Debt Collector 3M, Sly Deal 3M,
    Forced Deal 3M, Just Say No 4M, Deal Breaker 5M, Double The Rent 1M,
    House 3M, Hotel 4M.
  Money cards: 1M x6, 2M x5, 3M x3, 4M x3, 5M x2, 10M x1.
